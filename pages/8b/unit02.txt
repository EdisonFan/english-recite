5 Home safety
Many serious accidents happen in people’s homes. The kitchen and the bathroom 
are the two most dangerous places in the house. 
Can people prevent certain accidents? Yes. Many accidents happen because 
people are careless. So be careful!
Safety tips in the kitchen
●Be careful with fire and boiling water when you cook.
●Have fire blankets, fire extinguishers, smoke alarms, 
and flashlights near the kitchen.
●Always turn oﬀ  the oven or stove after use. In case 
of a gas leak, do not use a cell phone or any electric 
item. Immediately turn oﬀ  the stove and quickly open 
a window. Move to a safe area to inform emergency 
services and the gas company. 
Safety tips in the bathroom
●Never use electricity in the shower or bathtub. It can 
shock you!
●Be sure to keep the bathroom floor dry after you 
take a shower or bath. It’s easy to slip and fall on a wet 
floor. 
●Turn on the cold water first when taking a bath. 
Then adjust the hot water to ensure a safe water 
temperature. Hot water can burn you.
Home safety focuses on preventing accidents and 
reacting well to dangers in order to protect the safety of the family.
