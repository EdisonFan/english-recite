When we talk about spring, we think of 
a season of warm weather and fresh air. 
We also think of new life, green plants, 
and budding trees all around us. Spring 
is short in many places around the world. 
But in my hometown, it feels like spring 
nearly all year round. 
The weather here is neither too hot nor 
too cold. The average temperature in 
winter is 15°C. In summer, the average 
temperature is 24°C. While the sun 
is shining, the temperature remains 
pleasant. The city gets about 2,250 hours 
of sunshine and about 1,000 millimetres 
of rain every year. Because the weather 
is spring-like, you can find hundreds of 
beautiful flowers and trees all year round. 
3 The Spring City
There are many famous scenic spots 
in my hometown. The Stone Forest 
is a must-see for tourists from home 
and abroad. There are many special 
rocks that look like tall stone trees. 
The Dianchi Lake is the biggest in 
Yunnan Province. You can enjoy the 
beauty of the area and go boating or 
take a walk by the water.
Can you guess the name of my 
hometown? Yes, it’s Kunming — 
the Spring City. Welcome to my

The 24 solar terms reflect the wisdom of 
our ancestors. They help us understand 
how nature changes throughout the 
year. Our ancestors observed nature 
and created the Chinese lunar calendar 
to guide farming and daily life. There 
are several solar terms for the spring 
season. They tell us about the coming 
of this beautiful season.
Start of Spring
It usually falls around February 4 or 5 
and marks the beginning of spring. The 
weather starts to get warmer and we 
can see new plants grow and animals 
become active after a long winter.
Rain Water
It arrives around February 19 or 20. It 
reminds us of the increase in rainfall as 
the weather gets warmer.
Jingzhe (Awakening of Insects)
It usually takes place around March 
5 or 6. Jingzhe tells us that insects 
are coming out of hibernation. We 
might hear more birds singing and see 
butterflies and bees buzzing around.
Spring Equinox
It comes around March 20 or 21, when 
the length of the day is nearly equal to 
that of the night.
Qingming (Clear and Bright)
It usually falls around April 4 or 5. 
Qingming is a time when people 
honour their ancestors. It’s also a time 
to enjoy outdoor activities and the 
beauty of spring.
Grain Rain
It occurs around April 19 or 20. Farmers 
sow seeds during this time, and the 
rain nourishes the soil to make sure the 
farmers have a good harvest in the future. 
These solar terms teach us how to 
follow the seasons and make the best 
of them. They show us the relationship 
between humans and nature and call 
upon us to respect the world around us.
