means that it has gone through a process 
of heating to a certain temperature.
Jeremy: Why do we heat milk? 
Mum: Raw milk has dangerous bacteria 
and germs. We must heat milk to 72 
degrees Celsius for 15 seconds to kill them.
Jeremy: How did people discover that?
Mum: Many years ago, people 
sometimes got sick when they 
drank milk. Some of them even 
died because of the bad bacteria.
Jeremy: Wow! Who discovered this 
heating process? A dairy farmer or 
a scientist?
Mum: It was Louis Pasteur, a French 
microbiologist. He discovered the 
process around 1864.
Jeremy: That’s a long time ago!
Mum: (She nods and adds.) Mr 
Pasteur was one of the greatest 
scientists. His work saved millions 
of lives around the world. He 
was famous for his work on 
germs.
Jeremy: (He raises his glass 
of milk in the air.) Well, here’s 
a toast to you, Mr Pasteur.

world much safer.
